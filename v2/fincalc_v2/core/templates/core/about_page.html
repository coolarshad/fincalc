The FinanceToolsLab "Lab Standard"

Unlike simple approximation tools, every calculator deployed on FinanceToolsLab passes through a rigorous four-phase verification pipeline before public release.

01

Mathematical Rigor

We use the standard published formula for each tool (amortization, compound interest, Mifflin-St Jeor, US Navy method) and show it on the page.

02

Worked Examples

Each page includes a step-by-step example, recalculated by hand and checked against the calculator output.

03

Cited Sources

We link to the primary source behind each method, such as WHO, CFPB, BLS, ACOG or the original research paper.

04

Dated Updates

When rules change, such as India's GST rates in September 2025, we update the page and its "Updated" date.

How We Check Our Calculators

FinanceToolsLab.com is built and maintained by a small independent editorial and engineering team. We are not licensed financial advisers, tax professionals or clinicians, and nothing on this site is personal advice.

Every calculator uses a standard, published method, and we link to the primary source on each page: for example the World Health Organization for BMI, the Mifflin-St Jeor paper for calorie needs, ACOG for due dates and the CFPB for mortgage and debt guidance. Each worked example is recalculated by hand and checked against the calculator before the page is published, and the page shows the date it was last updated.

Found an error? Email support@financetoolslab.com and we will correct it and update the page date.
