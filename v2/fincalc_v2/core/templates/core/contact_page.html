Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Are all calculators 100% free to use?

Yes. All 20+ calculators and educational guides on FinanceToolsLab are completely free with no registration or subscriptions required.

Q. How do I report a formula discrepancy or bug?

Select "Report a Calculation Bug" in the form above, specify which calculator you were using, the exact inputs, and our editorial engineering team will audit it.

Q. Can I request a custom calculation tool?

Absolutely. We regularly build new calculation modules based on user demand. Select "Request New Calculator" to send us your specification.

Q. How fast will I receive a reply?

Our support and editorial team reviews incoming inquiries daily and typically responds within 24 business hours.
